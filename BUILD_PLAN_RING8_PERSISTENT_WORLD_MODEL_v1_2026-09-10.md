# Ring 8 Persistent World Model — Governing Build Plan

## Objective

Virelai converts the lossless interaction record into one persistent,
evidence-grounded, world-specific physics grammar. Duck consults that model so
established mechanics remain easy to remember, changes between levels stand out,
and observed wins can be described in the same object-and-rule language.

## Non-negotiable laws

1. Duck remains the only gameplay actor.
2. Raw frames and transitions are immutable evidence.
3. The canonical world model is the only semantic source of truth.
4. Every claim has a discrete status, counts, scope, and evidence references.
5. A visual candidate is never presented as an observed mechanic.
6. Cross-level correspondence is a hypothesis until behavior is reconfirmed.
7. No confidence floats.
8. The same evidence boundary must serialize to identical bytes.
9. Existing variants remain frozen.
10. Every rung passes its gate before its output becomes authoritative.

## Ordered ladder

1. Canonical schema and deterministic serialization.
2. Worldline and object-identity integration.
3. Evidence admission and contradiction handling.
4. Mechanics, state-variable, counter, and lever adapters.
5. Object-level win analysis.
6. Cross-level persistence and grammar diff.
7. Callable world, diff, object, mechanic, and evidence views.
8. Automatic transition briefing sourced only from the canonical model.
9. Recorded-fixture grading on vc33, ls20, and ft09.
10. Live vc33 validation.
11. Kaggle packaging and gameplay evaluation.

## Final evaluation

The decisive test is whether the accumulated model lets Duck treat level N+1 as
an extension of a known world: what persists, what changed, what is new, and how
the previous observed win constrains the next goal.
