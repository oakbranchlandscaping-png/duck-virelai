## North Star

Duck is an LLM-centered ARC investigator. Its pre-win goal is to explore the physics of the local
world through controlled interaction and stable record keeping. Intelligence is demonstrated by:

1. probing to understand the world;

The controller forces one narrow epistemic target at a time. Duck must identify, explain, predict,
and later use what it learned. Tests of schema compliance do not establish model use or semantic
understanding.

## Current gate doctrine

```text
G0 read action metadata and assign the control archetype
-> G1 locate and explain the ego and its direct control law
-> G2 identify scheduled bookkeeping noise and establish a reversible mask
-> G3 exhaustively locate all levers
-> G4 study each confirmed lever and its reactions in depth
-> G5 environmental forces
-> G6 scoped inertness
-> G7 counter-masked novel-state exploration
-> G8 observed-win explanation
-> G9 cross-level falsifiable transfer
```

CLICK worlds click every ranked visual object. NAV worlds use the demonstrated ego law to collide
or attempt contact with every ranked object. HYBRID worlds execute both ordered arms. The
controller owns the roster; positive discoveries do not stop coverage.

G2 closes when Duck explains a recurrent bookkeeping process, prospectively supports it, and the
controller can reversibly mask its footprint. The min/max consequence remains a background
obligation. Ordinary G3 probing may later expose death or respawn and lock that boundary field.

G3 asks only which objects are levers. A positive is repeated enough to establish attributable
causal identity and receive Duck's evidence-bound explanation. G4 asks how that lever works:
affected subjects, local/remote transformation, direction, magnitude, lag, reversibility,
saturation, preconditions, and falsifiers.

Any newly appearing visual object receives a persistent identity and an entity-specific G3
classification gate. If it proves to be a lever, it opens its own G4 subtree. G3 is therefore both
an opening sweep and an event-driven classifier throughout later exploration.
