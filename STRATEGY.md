## The strategy, whole

**1 · The physics grammar is deduced through targeted probing.**
The agent does not guess. It probes in lawful order — ego first (what covaries with
WHICH action), counter second (what ticks on ANY action; characterized, quarantined
from attribution, never unwatched), then the lever hunt (everything presumed a lever
until the world acquits it). Each press is chosen to reveal; pressing stops when
presses repeat. The library is built with the weave and the semantic roles: deltas →
objects → worldlines → verbs → mechanics → roles, each rung a compression of the one
below, every belief walkable back to the recorded deltas that earned it.

**2 · The win is found through exploration of new states.**
The root is the same — RESET returns the identical start state every time, for
everyone, so no branch is ever lost and deep frontiers stay cheaply reachable. The
frontier is a combination of actions — every untried action at every reached state is
an unvisited region of the same fixed graph. **The win must exist outside the seen
states:** every visited state is proven non-win by the run's own continuation, so the
win lives, by logic and not by hope, in what the map marks untried. Stale-state
tracking is therefore the soundness of the search itself. The grammar prunes the
frontier; the root makes it re-reachable; exploration exhausts it until a win is SEEN
— stumbled into, never guessed. (Corollary: an exhausted frontier with no win seen is
a sound verdict that the grammar is missing a variable — diagnostic, not defeat.)

**3 · Once found, the win is described in the terms of the physics grammar.**
Only an observed win can be described; only a described win can be attempted. The
description is a predicate over the grammar's objects and mechanics — what was
reached, aligned, consumed — never a memorized action sequence (the routes are a set;
the win is a state; a route is a witness, not the object).

**4 · The described win is attempted on the next world.**
The rule becomes a live hypothesis wherever it is next applicable — established
directly, in few presses, killed and re-learned where it fails.

## 5 · THE RHYTHM OF INDUCTION AND DEDUCTION (Director, 2026-08-26)

**LLMs are good pattern matchers. The Virelai upgrade probes and deduces the exact
pattern of each level. This is the rhythm of induction and deduction.**

The division, exactly: Virelai DEDUCES — exact facts from the record (visited states,
proven no-ops, seals: counter, ego, drain, lever), held without drift for the whole
game, which no language model can do. The LLM INDUCES — it is natively a pattern
completer, and completion is the leap deduction cannot make: from clean exact pieces
to "the pattern of this level is X." The loop alternates: probe → deduce the pieces →
hand them clean → the model completes the pattern → the completed pattern comes back
down as a rule the record can test (beat 5), and the next level kills it or keeps it.

Why the baseline fails without this: Tufa asked the pattern matcher to also be the
bookkeeper, so its inductions ran on drifting self-narrated inputs. Why the hybrid
works: induction runs on deduced ground truth. The golden thesis ("intelligence is
not pattern completion") and this instrument are consistent — pattern completion
CHAINED TO EXACT RULES is the hybrid's whole design, and each ring adds one more
exact piece for the completer to work with.

Build consequence, standing: every digest line and sealed read is written to be
INDUCTION FODDER — exact, compact, structured — and every ring is judged partly on
whether the model's stated world-model (its induced pattern) sharpens when the ring's
facts arrive.

## 6 · THE READING SURFACE: THE FIELD CARD (Director, 2026-08-26)

**The library owes the model a briefing, not only facts.** The original intent — *an
expandable field card, built by Virelai, used by an LLM* — is located in the tree at
`virelai_v4/library/field_card.py` ("a VIEW, not data"), and its scar is FRICTION-004:
it rendered every turn to a terminal log, a briefing prepared for a reader who never
came. **The hybrid's LLM is that reader.** Full finding:
`claude/field-card-finding-2026-08-26.md`.

Two thirds already exist here: the digest is the card's **pushed delta**; the `virelai`
sandbox variable is its **expandable body** (Memo №3 §4.2 — full structure queryable,
delta pushed). Not yet carried: the seven-section reading surface, roles grouped for a
reader, and **GAPS** — a rendered account of what Virelai does not yet know. The model
currently receives facts and never an account of the blindness around them, which is
half of honest briefing and is owed under typed silence.

**Ring 4 is chartered on that basis**, with four design constraints binding its
DESIGN.md — each one earned from a defect this project has already paid for:

1. **The card renders INSIDE the sandbox, as a pure function over `virelai`. It is
   never serialized into the payload.** "Costs nothing unread" is true only under this
   constraint: the sandbox is a fresh subprocess per tool call and the whole runtime
   state ships over stdin every call (duck-sweep F1/F2), so a pre-rendered card would
   be paid for on every call whether read or not. Unread must mean *unrendered*, not
   merely *unlooked-at*.
2. **Rendering may not compute.** v4's docstring promises a view that does not modify;
   the hybrid's `snapshot()` already calls `_evaluate_seals()` and `_rebuild_map()`.
   Seal evaluation belongs on the record path, never the read path. A view that
   advances belief on read is the thing the original docstring was guarding against.
3. **GAPS is typed silence, not a backlog.** Every gap line names the mechanism of the
   blindness ("no lever attribution exists on NAV worlds yet"). Open frontier is not a
   gap — untried actions are the FRONTIER section. Keeping the two apart is what stops
   GAPS becoming a dumping ground and losing its falsifiability.
4. **The fixture measures the READER, not the render.** FRICTION-004 was a proven
   artifact with no consumer; here the consumer is a model that must *choose* to call
   `virelai_card()`. If it never calls it, Ring 4 reproduces the scar inside the
   hybrid — a briefing nobody reads, one layer up. Render correctness is a unit-battery
   matter. The pre-registered fixtures are: **call rate per game**; whether the stated
   world model sharpens after a call (the §5 induction test, applied directly); and the
   pushed prompt word count.

5. **THE CARD'S INPUT IS OBSERVED, NOT CONCLUDED** (Director, 2026-08-27). Every line
   the card renders traces to a recorded observation and shows the evidence that earned
   it. The card reports **what was seen**, not a verdict the model is told to trust.

   *Earned the hard way, 2026-08-27.* Ring 3's ego seals told the model that an 80-cell
   4x20 bar (sp80), a 1-cell class-0 speck (ka59) and two class-0 blobs at opposite ends
   of the board (tr87) were **"YOU"** — permanent, with *"a press that does not move you
   means BLOCKED, not doubt."* Every game we named an ego in scored zero or lost its
   score. The model is not ignoring us; it complies exactly, which converts a perception
   defect straight into behaviour. A false conclusion plus *never doubt it* is an
   unfalsifiable instruction handed to an obedient reader.

   So the card says *"this 4-cell shape moved UP->(-1,0), DOWN->(+1,0) over 6 presses;
   nothing else moved with your presses"* and lets the reader conclude. Sealed reads may
   still be rendered — but as observations with their evidence attached, never as
   verdicts stripped of it, and never with a doubt prohibition. Where a seal is
   asserted, GAPS states what would falsify it.

   This constraint is also the ring's own measurement: the card's contribution is read by
   whether the model's stated world model sharpens after it arrives.

**Ring 4 is the first ring whose health metric should FALL.** Prompt grew 2,464 → 2,513
across the sealed-read contract build-out and was flagged honestly as a finding. The
card's charter is to absorb that vocabulary — push the delta, expand on demand — so
Ring 4 pre-registers the direction: **the pushed prompt gets shorter as the library
gets smarter.** A Ring 4 that renders beautifully and grows the prompt has failed its
own charter.
