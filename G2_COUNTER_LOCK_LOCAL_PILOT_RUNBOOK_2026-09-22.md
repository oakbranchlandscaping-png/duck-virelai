From `variants/ring9-cybernetic-gates/ARC3-Inference/` run:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=.:../tufa-arc-agi-framework/src \
CONFIG_PATH=configs/g2-counter-lock-live-pilot.json \
RUN_NAME=ring9-g2-counter-lock-local-ls20-v1 \
VIRELAI_MODE=gated \
make run
```

Grade G2 only after confirming the already-established G1 chain. Required G2 evidence:

1. G2 receives the demonstrated complete ego footprint.
2. Varied interventions produce a stable two-class full-bar candidate.
3. Duck explains its tracked variable, exchange law, bookkeeping basis, alternatives, and limits.
4. Duck's `COUNTER_REWRITE` prediction is accepted before the demonstrating action.
5. The demonstration is counter-only and supports the predicted rewrite and amount.
6. The mask covers the inferred full footprint and preserves raw grids.
7. G2 stays in `BOUNDARY_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` after rate support.
8. Game-over at the extremum or an extremal respawn is directly observed.
9. Only that observation creates `LOCKED_BY_EXTREMUM_BOUNDARY` and permits G3.

If the 48-action or runtime limit arrives first, grade the run `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. Do not call the
counter locked and do not infer the boundary consequence.
