## 1. Decision

The semantic library is the product and Duck's durable internal model of the world. The current Claude paper-evidence slice closes first under its existing acceptance contract. After that closure, the primary build objective becomes the ordered semantic program in this plan.

Push stays shelved. Free play remains a fallback. Score seeking does not displace library completion.

## 2. Accepted foundation

The accepted source already provides:

- tiered testing;
- schema-v4 canonical `world_model.json`;
- object census and transition-level descriptive ledger;
- the nine accepted observation verbs;
- compact transformation patterns, Duck digest, retrieval pages and `world_report.md`;
- gate-owned library agenda and selected G3 experiments in CLICK and NAV;
- fail-closed record checks;
- provenance capture and final reconciliation.

This is the D0 descriptive foundation. It is not yet a complete mechanics model. Current lever dossiers are `NOT_BUILT`; most product receipts retain hashes rather than the semantic content Duck needs; G5/G6 roles are unbuilt; the typed grammar draft is outside the runtime; observed-win transfer is unbuilt.

## 3. Locked build order

### Stage 0 — close the current Claude slice

Finish, review and either accept or reject the active PE-03 slice without expanding it into the mechanic build. Record its exact commit and test floor. The semantic sequence starts from that accepted base.

### Stage 1 — WO-G4-DOSSIER-A: durable per-lever mechanics

Create the next versioned world-model schema needed to retain the semantic content of accepted G4 products. One dossier exists for every certified lever, keyed by the G3 entity and joined to observation tracks where exact identity exists.

Separate chapters:

1. identity and activation;
2. effect anatomy;
3. repeated orbit or state sequence;
4. endpoints, persistence, reversal and limits;
5. relations to other levers and affected subjects;
6. prospective law tests;
7. contradictions, unknowns and next tests.

Each chapter has one of `NOT_BUILT`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `PRODUCT_PENDING`, `COMPLETE`, `CLOSED_UNRESOLVED` or `INTERRUPTED`. Every factual field resolves to gate products, predictions and receipts. The canonical model stores normalized semantic content, not only payload hashes.

### Stage 2 — WO-G4-DOSSIER-B: library-selected study

The first incomplete actionable dossier chapter becomes the selected library question. Mandatory gate obligations retain priority. Duck may select among eligible lever questions on turns it already receives; the controller never waits and resumes deterministic coverage after decline or timeout.

The card, digest, capability, selected probe, route, execution, receipt and updated dossier must identify the same subject and question. Completing one lever cannot close another lever's dossier.

### Stage 3 — WO-NOUN-CONTINUITY: robust subject identity

Strengthen level-scoped identity before forces, driven roles or grammar depend on it:

- accept exact structural `GROW` and `SHRINK` joins when the predecessor is unique and unconsumed;
- keep `DEFORM` ambiguous unless an accepted structural rule proves the join;
- preserve explicit lineage and candidate links;
- never transfer a closed question across an unresolved join;
- retain historical receipts when a class change ends a binding;
- grade continuity against fixed labelled cases with zero false joins.

G3 entity IDs remain the dossier subjects while track joins remain explicit and revisable.

### Stage 4 — WO-G5 / WO-DRIVEN: remaining active nouns

After subtracting certified ego, counter and lever effects:

- certify an `ENVIRONMENTAL_FORCE` only from recurring residual dynamics with an explicit schedule and trigger scope;
- certify `REACTIVE_DRIVEN` only from an incoming dependency tied to a certified driver;
- record multiple-driver, lag, magnitude, direction, period and terminal-candidate questions;
- keep recurrence without attribution as residual evidence.

Port pure measurements and structural predicates from older systems. Do not port their writers, thresholds or role verdicts.

### Stage 5 — WO-G6: scoped negatives and inertness

Represent what was shown not to happen. Each negative names subject, action class, board or state scope, conditions, tested interval and evidence.

`INERT` requires exhaustive coverage over its declared scope and exclusion of every active role. A silent single probe remains a scoped null, never inertness.

### Stage 6 — WO-GRAMMAR: executable physics sentences

Compile immutable typed expressions from certified nouns, observation events, relations, conditions and gate products. Each sentence includes:

- subject, predicate and affected object or value;
- scope and temporal form;
- grounding references;
- epistemic and prospective status;
- competing models and a falsifier;
- deterministic serialization and content identity.

Laws require a supported prospective test or remain pending, contradicted or unresolved. Prose is rendered from typed meaning and never parsed back into belief. Grammar sentences live as a chapter of `world_model.json`, not a second grammar artifact.

### Stage 7 — WO-WIN-TRANSFER: describe, freeze and test the win

Preserve the actual winning render in the canonical record. Freeze the complete pre-win library. Describe the terminal transition using only existing nouns, verbs, relations and conditions. Separate the final action from the earlier configuration that enabled success.

The first observed separator remains a novelty-bounded hypothesis. Lift surviving expressions by type, freeze them at next-level entry and grade them prospectively. Cross-level resemblance never certifies identity by itself.

### Stage 8 — recovery, evidence and release

Use the running Kaggle save run as a baseline of the accepted pre-dossier system. After the semantic stages land, run the fixed public-25 evidence protocol from committed source. Repair runtime friction that prevents the library ladder from progressing. Rehearse and release under the competition freeze directive.

## 4. Parallel lane

Runtime reliability may proceed alongside semantic work only where files are disjoint. NAV G1 stops, repair loops and bounded submission context restart may not weaken evidence admission, reorder the semantic stages or create another belief writer.

PE-03 claims and grading remain a measurement layer over the library. They may expose weaknesses, but they do not replace the semantic product or define its ontology.

## 5. Required measurements

Every evidence run reports, per world and level:

- observed, active, stopped, crashed and cleared status;
- object and transition coverage;
- exact, unresolved and broken identity counts;
- observation verbs and transformation patterns;
- explained, partly explained and unexplained event counts;
- certified roles by archetype;
- lever dossier completion by chapter;
- supported, contradicted, indeterminate and pending predictions;
- scoped negatives and inert certificates;
- selected questions, Duck choices, handoffs and controller fallback;
- model, digest and report latency and size;
- provenance integrity and completeness;
- observed wins and prospective next-level verdicts.

## 6. Global invariants

1. The gate machine is the only certifier.
2. `world_model.json` is the only canonical library.
3. Observed change is not causality.
4. A lever witness is not a repeated mechanic law.
5. A recurring footprint is not persistent identity.
6. A silent probe is not inertness.
7. A first win separator is not a cause.
8. `UNKNOWN` and `INCONCLUSIVE` are never rewritten as false.
9. Later evidence may grade a frozen prediction but may not edit it.
10. Any record, identity or provenance break fails the dependent claim closed while preserving unaffected history.

## 7. Program exit

The program closes when Duck can enter a novel world, build a detailed evidence-backed world machine, use its open questions to direct study, express supported mechanics as executable sentences, describe an observed win without new vocabulary, and test that frozen description prospectively on the next level.
