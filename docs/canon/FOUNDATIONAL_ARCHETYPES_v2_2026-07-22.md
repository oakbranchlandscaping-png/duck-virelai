## 0 · WHAT AN ARCHETYPE IS

An archetype is a **role hypothesis with three attachments**: a **defining observable** (the delta-pattern that asserts it — per the Grounding Law, a predicate over recorded deltas and measured mechanics, decidable from the record), an **exclusion discipline** (what its identification removes from other searches — the noise-accounting that makes the lawful progression work), and a **subtree seed** (the opening question-list its commitment dispatches, deepened per `PROBE_STRATEGY_v2` with three-valued child dispositions: closed-by-data / closed-by-probe / deferred).

Roles are compressions of mechanics; an archetype earns commitment only through its observable, never through appearance, position, size, or any perceptual prior. All archetypes attach to worldlines — the worldline is the object; the archetype is what the worldline's integrated record says it *does*.

The six: **EGO · COUNTER · LEVER · ENVIRONMENTAL FORCE · REACTIVE-DRIVEN · INERT.**

---

## 1 · EGO

**What it is.** The self — the worldline whose changes are the agent's own actions landing in the world.

**Defining observable.** **Action-covariance:** the worldline's delta stream covaries with the agent's own action stream, per-action, above chance — moves when and only as acted, with the mapping stable across the record. Ego-ness is covariance with *own* actions, nothing else.

**Modalities.** *Embodied:* a footprint on the grid whose kinematics track the action set. *Null-locus:* on worlds where the acting self has no body (the GOD_MODE modality), the ego binds with an empty footprint — the covariance is between actions and world-changes-at-selected-sites rather than a body's motion. The archetype is one; the locus is a modality attribute.

**Position in the progression — first, absolutely.** The ego is the loudest noise source in the record: everything it does is self-caused. Until it is identified, its motion masquerades as world response. **Ego identification is the precondition of every causal read**, which is why the progression opens here and why the ego is excluded from lever discovery the moment it commits.

**Subtree seed.** Kinematics (the action→delta mapping, per action); locus and extent (footprint, or null); collision and constraint (what stops it, what it cannot enter); carry and contact effects (what changes when it touches things — the seam where ego probing hands findings to lever discovery); persistence discipline (what survives its death — the life-boundary behavior of its worldline).

---

## 2 · COUNTER

**What it is.** A bookkeeper — a worldline that tracks a quantity on its own schedule.

**Defining observable.** **Schedule-autonomy with monotone structure:** the delta stream is regular against the action count or the frame count (a stable per-action or per-frame rate), monotone between boundary events, and its resets align with structural boundaries (life, level) rather than with any particular world state. It ticks because time passed or actions were spent — not because of *which* action.

**Position in the progression — second.** The counter is the second noise source: it changes every press regardless of what was pressed, so until identified, every lever probe reads its tick as an effect. Identified, it is excluded from lever discovery and its rate becomes a *subtraction term* — the known component removed from every subsequent delta read.

**Subtree seed.** Rate (per-action vs per-frame, and the measured coefficient); direction and range (depletion or accumulation; observed extrema); reset law (which boundaries reset it, to what value); coupling audit (is the rate ever modulated by anything — a counter whose rate responds to a lever is a coupled mechanic, and the finding routes to the causal map); consequence hypothesis (what its extremum coincides with in the record — a boundary event at zero is a budget candidate, held as candidate).

---

## 3 · LEVER

**What it is.** A cause — a worldline acting-on-which changes *other* worldlines.

**Defining observable.** **Attributed exogenous effect:** with ego and counter identified and excluded, a press attributed to this worldline (the acted-member attribution, `SPEC_MECHANIC_LAYER` §4) correlates with deltas on other worldlines — an effect that survives subtraction of the ego's own motion and the counter's tick. The lever is the archetype that *cannot be soundly identified first*; its observable is defined over the residue the foundation roles leave.

**Cross-branch.** The only role assertable under both Band-1 parents: a **remote lever** is STATIC (it never changes; its effects land elsewhere); a reactive lever changes visibly while causing. The observable is the same — attributed exogenous effect; self-delta is irrelevant to lever-ness.

**Position in the progression — third, and the point of the first two.** The whole noise-accounting order exists so that this identification is sound.

**Subtree seed.** Mechanism (which action modality operates it); effect subjects (which worldlines respond — the causal-map edges); effect quality, bearing, and magnitude (the measured marginal, per subject); locality (site-bound or field); coupling membership (does its effect participate in a conserved transfer — routes to the mechanic stratum); repetition law (identical presses, identical effects? — determinism check per press, any divergence a fork-witness for hidden state); saturation (does the effect clamp at a wall — the computed-residual discipline).

## 4 · ENVIRONMENTAL FORCE

**What it is.** The world's own dynamics — a worldline that changes autonomously, on the world's initiative rather than the agent's or a schedule's.

**Defining observable.** **Autonomous, non-scheduled change:** a delta stream uncorrelated with the agent's action stream (fails the ego test) and without stable rate structure (fails the counter test), yet real and recurring in the record. The archetype of the residual dynamics — what still moves when self and clock are subtracted.

**Position in the progression.** Identified by elimination as the foundation roles commit; a standing confound for lever attribution (its autonomous changes can coincide with presses), which is why its deltas are flagged and discounted in effect attribution once its pattern is characterized.

**Subtree seed.** Trigger structure (is the "autonomy" actually conditioned on a world state — a force that fires when a configuration holds is a mechanic with a state predicate, and the finding sharpens it); periodicity audit (distinguishing a complex schedule from true state-dependence); interaction (does it act on the ego — a hazard candidate — or on other worldlines); conservation membership.

*Marker — reconstruction:* this archetype's name is directly committed (`SOLVER_BLUEPRINT_v3` §5); its observable is stated here by the elimination structure the progression implies. The defining observable clause is reconstruction at that altitude — flag any surviving v1 session detail that states it differently.

---

## 5 · REACTIVE-DRIVEN

**What it is.** An effect — a worldline whose changes are *caused from elsewhere*: the dependent member of the causal map.

**Defining observable.** **Dependent membership in a measured coupling:** its deltas are predicted by another worldline's deltas or by attributed presses on another worldline — it is the derived complement, or a subject on a lever's effect edge (`SPEC_MECHANIC_LAYER` §5). It moves, but the explanation of its motion lives on another worldline's record.

**Position in the progression.** Emerges from lever deepening and coupling identification — the archetype is the *answer* to "what does the lever act on," never a first-pass guess. Committing it closes attribution loops: a driven object's deltas stop being unexplained residue.

**Subtree seed.** Drivers (the full set of edges into it); response law (the fitted dependence, per driver — quality, magnitude, lag); exclusivity (driven only, or does it also cause — driven+lever is a legal set-valued pair, a relay); terminal candidacy (does the record associate its states with boundary events — a win-predicate ingredient, held as candidate behind the win-wall).

---

## 6 · INERT

**What it is.** The honest null — a worldline that, under exhaustive interaction, does nothing and causes nothing.

**Defining observable.** **Coverage-bounded silence:** no self-delta (STATIC parent) and no attributed effect across the *enumerated* interaction set — every applicable action tried at it, nothing moved anywhere. The observable is as much about the coverage as the silence: inert is never assertable from quiet alone; it is assertable from *tried-everything* quiet.

**Laws, both absolute.** **Sole-exclusive:** INERT co-exists with no other tag — it is the claim that no other tag applies. **Derivation-only and reopenable:** a coverage-bounded verdict, never permanent — a black swan (a new modality, a changed world phase) reopens it without ceremony. The inert tag is the taxonomy's most drainable bucket by design.

**Position in the progression.** Last — the residue of everything else failing to bind, affordable only after the enumeration completes.

**Subtree seed.** Deliberately shallow: coverage certificate (which actions were tried, when — the evidence the tag stands on); reopen triggers (what events void the certificate); SIGNAL watch — the queued child (`NESTED_TAXONOMY_SPEC_v5` §6): transient-witness observations (flashes on contact, informative blinks) recorded beside the tag, accumulating toward the chartered SIGNAL subtree — inert in effect, informative in transient.

## 7 · THE SET-VALUED READ, RESTATED FROM THE LEAVES

The archetypes are not mutually exclusive except where a law says so: ego+lever is legal (a self that causes), driven+lever is legal (a relay); INERT excludes all. Every commitment is class-indexed — a class transformation revokes and re-derives the whole tag-set. Every observable above decompresses to predicates over recorded deltas and measured mechanics; there is no archetype whose test requires seeing what the record cannot show. That is what "axiomatic" means here, and it is the whole warrant for the key: any object, walked through these observables in the lawful order, terminates — and the path it took *is* its dossier's skeleton.
