## 0 · WHAT THE TAXONOMY IS

The taxonomy is the solver's **identification key**: a nested tree of questions that discerns any object step by step, the way a dichotomous key determines any specimen. Its two defining properties:

**Completeness.** Every object terminates somewhere. No-null-class forces this: nothing is a non-object, so nothing is a non-role. UNKNOWN is *absence of evidence* — a bucket still filling — never a terminal leaf.

**Observable branches.** Every split is answered by an observable — a recorded delta pattern, a measured mechanic, a probe response — never by intuition or appearance. Per the Grounding Law, every branch question decompresses to a predicate over cell deltas. The archetypes are axiomatic exactly insofar as the top-level partition tests are always collectable: the branch *is* a probe.

The taxonomy is **not flat and not orthogonal.** The flat-five (v1-era) and orthogonal-bands (v2-era) forms are retired; chambers have re-derived them repeatedly from unamended documents, and this spec exists partly to end that. The form is nested: a parent observation prunes which children are even assertable.

## 1 · BAND 1 — SELF-STATE, THE PARENT TAG

The first observation, pure and belief-free, read off a settled frame: **does this object change?**

- **STATIC** — no self-delta observed across the settled record.
- **REACTIVE** — self-delta observed.

Band 1 is earned by observation alone, before any probing, and it does structural work: **it prunes the assertable role menu by value.**

- STATIC → assertable children: **{ LEVER (remote / field), INERT }**. A thing that never changes can still *cause* change elsewhere (a remote lever) or cause nothing (inert); it cannot be an ego, a counter, or driven.
- REACTIVE → assertable children: **{ EGO, LEVER, COUNTER, ENVIRONMENTAL FORCE, REACTIVE-DRIVEN }**.

## 2 · BAND 2 — ROLE, THE DERIVED CHILDREN

The role tag is **inferred from the delta pattern** — read off the mechanic stratum (marginals and couplings, `SPEC_MECHANIC_LAYER`), never off appearance. The six archetypes (detailed per-archetype in `FOUNDATIONAL_ARCHETYPES_v2`):

**EGO · LEVER · COUNTER · ENVIRONMENTAL FORCE · REACTIVE-DRIVEN · INERT**

Laws of the tag, all ratified:

- **Set-valued.** The role tag is a non-empty subset of the assertable menu, not a single label. Overlaps are legal where evidence supports them — ego+lever is a legal pair. **INERT is sole-exclusive**: it co-exists with nothing, because it is the claim that nothing else applies.
- **Class-indexed.** The tag-set is indexed to the object's class signature. A class transformation **revokes and re-derives the entire tag-set** — earned belief does not survive the evidence that earned it changing identity. (The bucket-drain law applied to the taxonomy.)
- **Derived, never stored.** Per the standing law: the tag is a compression event over integrated evidence, recomputed against the mechanic stratum, never a latched authority.
- **LEVER is the cross-branch role** — the only child assertable under both Band-1 parents (a static remote lever; a reactive lever that also visibly changes).
- **EGO admits null-locus** — on worlds where the acting self has no body on the grid (the GOD_MODE modality), the ego archetype binds with an empty footprint; ego-ness is action-covariance, not embodiment.

## 3 · THE ANIMATION LAW

Animation splits by **settled outcome**, and the split protects Band 1 from transient noise:

- A **persistent transformation** — the object settles changed — is a true Band-1 REACTIVE witness.
- A **transient excursion** (flash, blink, brief overlay that settles back) **never flips a Band-1 tag.** It is recorded as an animation observation beside the settled tag, never merged into it.

Transients are evidence — of feedback, of signaling — but they are evidence *for the subtree*, not for the parent partition.

## 4 · COMPOSITE AGGREGATION — THE ∃/∀ LAW

Where an object is composite — a whole whose members are individuated — its tag evidence aggregates from members under explicit quantifiers: some claims are existential (one member's witness suffices to assert the property of the whole) and some are universal (the claim holds of the whole only if it holds of every member; one counter-witness kills it). The aggregation quantifier is part of each claim's definition, never left implicit — an ∃-claim graded as ∀ over-kills, and a ∀-claim graded as ∃ over-asserts.

*Marker — reconstruction:* the ∃/∀ aggregation law is named as ratified-and-owed in the committed open-question ledger; the per-tag quantifier table from the ratifying session was not recoverable from committed strata. The law is stated here at the altitude the ledger supports; **the per-tag table is owed as v5.1 or an amendment**, from the Director's session record or fresh ratification. This gap is declared, not papered.

## 5 · THE NESTED DEEPENING — TAG → CHILDREN → SUBTREES

The full descent, matching the ratified build-target clause ("through the nested taxonomy"):

**Band-1 tag → assertable role children → per-role subtrees, deepened asymmetrically.**

Each committed role opens its archetype's subtree (`FOUNDATIONAL_ARCHETYPES_v2`, `PROBE_STRATEGY_v2`): the nested question-list whose answers fill the object's dossier. Deepening is **asymmetric by realized yield** — high-yield objects saturate slowly and are probed deeper; low-yield objects saturate fast and are left. Every child question carries a three-valued disposition — **closed-by-data, closed-by-probe, deferred** — so gated depth is designed in, never designed out: a question the passive record already answers is closed without spending an action; a question it cannot answer is probed; a question gated behind an unreached state is deferred honestly, not marked done.

The taxonomy's branches and the descent through them are one and the same structure: walking deeper into the key *is* descending to finer resolution. Where a branch's answer goes stale or ambiguous, the finer question is the branch's own child — descent falls out of the key's structure.

## 6 · GROWTH — HOW THE TREE EXTENDS

The taxonomy grows by **chartered subtree campaign, never ad hoc.** A recurring pattern that no leaf names — a stable residual at some branch — is the evidence that a child node is owed; the child opens by charter, with its defining observable stated before it enters the tree. First queued instance, ratified: INERT will gain a **SIGNAL** child (the flash-witness as discriminator — an object inert in effect but informative in transient), opening via its own chartered campaign.

Vocabulary residue, ratified dispositions: **TARGET derives** (a target is read from mechanics and the win-predicate, never a stored primitive); **UNKNOWN is absence** (never a leaf, never sole-terminal); **INERT is derivation-only** (asserted only by exhaustive coverage, and reopenable — a coverage-bounded black swan, never a permanent verdict).

## 7 · THE SPEC IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The taxonomy is a dichotomous key over objects: Band 1 observes whether a thing changes (STATIC/REACTIVE) and prunes which roles are even assertable; Band 2 derives a set-valued, class-indexed role tag from the mechanic stratum — six archetypes, LEVER cross-branch, EGO null-locus legal, INERT sole-exclusive; animation transients never flip the parent tag; composite claims aggregate under explicit ∃/∀ quantifiers; each committed role opens its archetype's subtree, deepened asymmetrically by yield with three-valued child dispositions; and the tree grows only by chartered campaign when a residual proves a child is owed. Every branch is an observable; every path terminates; nothing is assumed and nothing is unnameable.
