Date: 2026-09-19  
Applies to: Ring 9 cybernetic gate family

## What G1 is designed to provide

G1 answers one question:

> Where and how can Duck directly influence this world?

It begins only after G0 has recorded the exact opaque action surface. It treats action numbers as
interfaces, not meanings.

G1's controller provides a bounded scientific protocol:

1. execute every offered non-coordinate action as a consecutive pair;
2. for coordinate action `ACTION6`, probe deterministic representative classes derived from the
   visible frame;
3. contrast a positive coordinate class and expand a class only when members disagree;
4. preserve local, remote, scheduled, terminal, and unavailable-action observations separately;
5. stop at a typed representation gap when no visible discriminating effect exists;
6. forbid inertness claims from negative G1 probes;
7. require Duck to explain the control mode, controlled subject or locus, and opaque action laws;
8. freeze Duck's scoped prediction before its demonstration action;
9. compare the later observation only inside the declared scope while reporting remote change
   separately;
10. create a stable, versioned `EgoLaw` only after a supported demonstration.

A completed `EgoLaw` contains:

- control mode, such as embodied, pointer-like, god-mode, mixed, or conditional;
- the visible subject or locus Duck claims to control;
- one or more opaque action laws;
- conditions and constraints;
- direct evidence references;
- competing explanations and a falsifier;
- limitations;
- the supported demonstration reference and witness transition;
- stable law and version identifiers for later gates.

G1 does not identify a counter, remove bookkeeping noise, infer a lever, explain reactions, prove an
environmental force, certify inert objects, search counter-masked novelty, infer a win condition, or
transfer a goal across levels. Remote change observed during an ego probe remains evidence for a
later lever/reaction investigation; it is not automatically part of the ego law.

## What counts as G1 satisfaction

For each required checkpoint world, all six H5 grades must pass:

1. **Protocol:** G0 preceded G1; all opaque probes have exact event and lossless-outcome joins; raw
   review frames exist.
2. **Coverage:** the H4 certificate shows all required pairs/classes completed, coverage stopped on
   a discriminating effect, and unknown members were not called inert.
3. **Explanation:** Duck names a control mode and subject, explains action laws and mechanism,
   cites direct evidence, and preserves an alternative plus falsifier.
4. **Prediction:** the action, scope, predicate, rationale, and novelty were journaled before a
   later transition.
5. **Demonstration:** the scoped verifier supports the exact prospective claim and produces a
   versioned observed `EgoLaw`.
6. **Correctness:** a human inspecting the receipt-bound frames confirms the ego identity, action
   laws, genuine prospectivity, scope, and absence of counter/remote misattribution.

Schema completion, confident prose, a whole-board change, or an automated supported predicate does
not substitute for the correctness grade. Later use of the law is useful telemetry but is not part
of G1 closure.

## When a world may move to G2

A world may enter G2 only after its G1 prerequisite closes with the required product. Engineering
may implement and package the dependent gate earlier so a single live checkpoint can expose the
handoff, stopping behavior, and later-gate friction. Evaluation remains per gate and per world:
an unreached G2 is `NOT_REACHED`, not a G2 failure, and integrated code does not imply that G1 or
G2 has passed live review.

If a world ends at `REPRESENTATION_GAP`, exhausts its action/runtime budget before a
demonstration, produces an indeterminate or contradicted comparison, or fails human correctness,
G1 remains open. The trace should be used to repair G1's representation, probe policy, explanation
contract, or verifier before adding the counter gate.

Opening G2 does not require observing a win or solving a level. It requires a trustworthy
`EgoLaw`, because G2 must distinguish changes caused directly by Duck from changes that represent
time, budget, score, progress, or other bookkeeping dynamics.

## G2's first dependency

G2 will consume the demonstrated `EgoLaw` and the same lossless flipbook evidence. Its narrow
question will be:

> Which changing visible structure is bookkeeping noise, what variable does it track, and how can
> it be reversibly masked without erasing causal world state?

Its required product is a versioned `CounterModel` plus a reversible `MaskLaw`. The G2 evaluator
must reject any trace that lacks a valid G1 prerequisite, preventing ego motion or remote causal
effects from being certified as bookkeeping noise.
