## 2 · What the sweep says about the thesis

1. **"Nearly everything can be offloaded" is not a conjecture; it is the lab's own history.** Each row above has a module. The ordered inquiry has been built three times. The paper's lineage section writes itself from this table, and it is a stronger Novelty claim than "we thought of gates": the lab iterated the idea across four architectures before the frontier papers appeared.
2. **The recurring failure is the consumer, not the design.** v3.5: *"Stream B drives nothing"*, the crucible ran zero times. v4: 17 contracts, 2,176 lines, 133 passing tests, zero readers; the ownership map graded zero of ten functions SHIPPED. Ring 9 is the first wiring that produced a live win. Every port in LW-A–E must land with its consumer named in the slice, or the fifth copy joins the pile.
3. **Two rows have no prior at all:** the hidden-state test and the relation predicate set. Both are small. Both close holes named in the thesis table.
4. **One row was dropped and should come back:** autonomous change. v3.5's agent detection is the port source; Ring 9's residual attribution over the record is the shape. It is the archetype the thesis declares unknown.
5. **The golden run is the pure-deterministic pole's best evidence, and it is one month old.** Rungs 0–3 closed live on ls20 and vc33 with no model. The paper should cite it as the lower bound: this far, code alone; past the win, the model is handed the result.
