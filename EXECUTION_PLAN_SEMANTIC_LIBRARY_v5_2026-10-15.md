## 3. Dossier minimum contract

Each lever dossier contains:

- stable entity ID and optional exact track join;
- observed appearance and current state;
- activation method, actor and reachability;
- local and remote affected subjects or unresolved regions;
- exact transformation events;
- repetition sequence, period and phase when supported;
- endpoint, saturation, persistence and reversal observations;
- relations to other levers and subjects;
- predictions with declared board, action, expected effects, outcome and contradictions;
- conditions, limitations, open questions and next falsifying experiment;
- chapter status and typed evidence references.

No aggregate product may hide a partially completed lever. Early stops must retain every completed per-lever chapter and explicitly mark unfinished chapters.

## 4. Study-selection contract

The agenda selects the earliest incomplete actionable chapter, ordered within each lever as:

1. anatomy;
2. orbit;
3. limits;
4. relations;
5. law test.

Gate obligations such as pending confirmation, reachability or an in-progress pursuit remain first. Duck may choose another eligible subject only through the gate's admission contract. A timeout or decline consumes no experiment and returns to controller coverage.

## 5. Continuity contract

Continuity is structural and fail-closed:

- exact same footprint with changed class supports RECOLOR;
- exact translation with same class supports MOVE;
- unique strict subset or superset with the same class supports SHRINK or GROW;
- ambiguous overlap produces a candidate link, never a durable join;
- split, merge, remint and respawn require explicit lineage evidence;
- lives and levels remain separate scopes unless a named carry rule is prospectively reconfirmed.

Every joined identity decompresses to its sightings, and all cells remain covered and disjoint at each verified node.

## 6. Later-role contracts

### Force

A recurring residual after known effects are removed, with explicit timing, trigger scope, affected subjects and evidence separating it from a counter or lever.

### Reactive-driven

An incoming measured dependency from a certified driver, with response type, magnitude, lag, period and evidence.

### Negative

A statement of what did not happen under a named subject, action, state, condition, interval and evidence scope.

### Inert

An exhaustive negative certificate over the declared coverage space, with every active role excluded.

## 7. Grammar contract

The grammar compiler is pure and read-only. It emits typed expressions from validated model content.

Minimum forms:

- state predicates;
- action-conditioned transformations;
- driver/affected-subject relations;
- finite conditions and negation;
- conjunction and ordered `THEN` clauses;
- existential and universal quantification over an explicitly complete subject set.

Evaluation is three-valued: `TRUE`, `FALSE` or `UNKNOWN`. It returns evidence and a missing-information reason. Unknown syntax or incomplete required inputs are refused.

## 8. Win-transfer contract

The canonical record preserves decision board, action, winning result board and successor spawn separately. The win sentence receives no terminal label as an input. The referee compares it afterward against observed terminal and comparable non-terminal transitions.

Verdicts remain `ADMITTED`, `KILLED`, `UNRESOLVED` or `VACUOUS`; admitted means survived observed comparisons, not proven causal.
