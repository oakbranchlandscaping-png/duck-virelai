## Non-negotiable research rules

- A model statement is not evidence merely because a language model wrote it.
- The gate owns admission and certification; receipts and stored records support conclusions.
- Evidence does not cross games, levels, or lives without an explicit bridge.
- Unknown, unresolved, refused, and unverified are distinct states.
- Failed runs and superseded plans remain visible when they explain the design.
