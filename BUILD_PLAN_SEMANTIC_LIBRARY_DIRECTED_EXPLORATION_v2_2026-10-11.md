## 1. Destination and operating rule

The product is a **versioned, evidence-backed semantic library for each novel world and each level observed in it**. It is Duck's working model of the world: the thing it reads to decide which uncertainty to investigate next. The canonical persisted artifact is the per-game `world_model.json`, now schema v2 in `world_model_v2.py`, compiled from the gate record by `library_census.py`. `world_report.md` is a human rendering of that same validated model, never a second belief store. The gate record remains the source of certified belief; the live view is a bounded, validated in-memory projection that cannot be saved as the canonical file. The library identifies observed objects, supported roles, dynamics, causal relations, and precisely typed unknowns. Its value is independent of a level clear.

The Push as an actor, win-seeking action selection, and the Push handoff are **shelved**. Passive record analysis may continue where it directly improves the library. When no gate can act, the fallback keeps Duck oriented toward an **actionable library gap** and records its moves with `FREE_PLAY` receipts. The current `PLAY-NORMAL` wording still says to win the level; revise that wording and its tests to match this directive. A gap marked `NOT_BUILT` is not an experiment Duck can perform. No free-play transition is promoted into gated evidence by relabelling it later. R-2's paper-evidence boundary remains in force.

**Control loop:** the library identifies the most relevant open knowledge target; the active gate translates it into admitted experiments; the controller takes routine evidence-gathering steps; Duck chooses among bounded experiments where judgment helps; Virelai records the result; deterministic code certifies only supported conclusions and updates the library; Duck reads that revision before choosing again. The model may propose a hypothesis or action, but its assertion never writes certified belief. The card and library must agree on the current target; the card alone grants permission to act.

In the current code, **G1 identifies the ego/control locus and G2 identifies the counter** (the Director's conversational numbering put the counter first; no gate renumbering is implied). G3 establishes a roster of witnessed levers: one attributed activation with masked residue admits a lever. G4 reads that roster and studies **each** lever's states, effects, mechanism and conditions. G5/G6 and the remaining taxonomy cover forces, driven reactions and scoped negatives. The earliest **actionable** gate is the current goal; a missing prerequisite never becomes a certified downstream product.

**Observed-win loop:** probing the level may reveal a win. Record its before-board, action, after-board, involved visual objects, level/life, and receipt. Describe the observed level-1 win in a typed sentence of visual objects, verbs and conditions, separating what the record shows from what Duck conjectures. Reuse the existing level freeze and carry ledger as evidence containers; they do not certify cross-level identity. Freeze the sentence **before** testing level 2. On level 2, identify candidate corresponding objects as hypotheses, predict what the sentence would imply there, and perform an admitted comparable test. Grade success, failure or non-comparability; narrow or revoke a failed claim. This is learning from an observed win, not a policy that drives Duck to win or a return of the Push.

## 2. What the library contains

One per-world manifest names the world identity and every independent run; evidence stays scoped to its game, level and life. Use an authoritative world ID when the runner supplies one. Otherwise, group exact opening-board and action-roster matches under a **provisional** fingerprint for reporting only; identical openings do not prove identical mechanics. A clone may suggest a hypothesis for another run, but it cannot donate that run's verdict. Each level has a frozen library revision at its boundary and a current revision while play continues.

| Library component | Minimum content | Source and rule |
|---|---|---|
| Object census | Every observed worldline, present or departed, once; sightings, class signature and change history | Read-only replay from the level-entry board and complete transition record, including early-stop paths; G3 entity IDs join only when exact and one-to-one; compiled into `world_model.json` |
| Parent and roles | Band-1 STATIC, REACTIVE or unsettled; set-valued roles among EGO, COUNTER, LEVER, ENVIRONMENTAL FORCE, REACTIVE-DRIVEN and INERT | Each role has evidence and scope. A class change revokes the old role set. INERT is exclusive and needs a coverage certificate. UNKNOWN is a gap, never a role. |
| Dossiers | For every supported role: witnessed states, interventions, outcomes, conditions, limitations, contradictions and next questions | G1/G2 products and G3–G6 records. One attributed activation with masked residue enters a lever at G3; G4 adds repeat behavior, orbit, mechanism and conditions. |
| Laws | Typed, immutable sentence; subject/class; conditions; predicted or explained change; evidence references; test status | Prose renders the typed claim. Grounding and prospective checks are distinct; the win referee grades win sentences only. |
| Observed wins and transfer | The winning transition and its visual context; a typed win sentence; a frozen level-N hypothesis; level-(N+1) object matches, prediction, test and verdict | A clear is an observed event. Cross-level identity and mechanics are hypotheses until tested in the new level. No later level's evidence enters the frozen sentence. |
| Gaps and residuals | Unanswered archetype questions, why open, budget and reachability limits, unexplained changes | Explicitly distinguishes not tested, inconclusive, contradicted and impossible to test under the current scope. |
| Provenance | Action chooser, admitting gate, GATED/FREE_PLAY receipt, bound target, before/action/result frames, level/life and source transition IDs | Certification checks provenance before using a transition. No cross-game or cross-level evidence leak. |

The card shows a compact **library digest**, not the full artifact: the current knowledge target; the gate's admitted choice when Duck is needed; newly certified findings; the roster of levers and which dossiers remain unexplained; contradictions and top **actionable** gaps; remaining budget. A bounded retrieval tool serves the full object, dossier, evidence, win and transfer pages. A new library revision is shown after a change; unchanged prose is not repeated on every turn. The current view slice may append a separate digest after the unchanged binding card, provided the whole rendered digest has a hard size limit and every retrieval response has a continuation path. Measure total prompt size and Duck turn latency, not just card size. The canonical JSON has one versioned schema and validator; extend it explicitly for new chapters and retain a migration reader. Render the human report from the validated JSON.

## 3. Honest completion states

These statuses are emitted per level and aggregated per novel world. They are not synonyms.

1. **ACCOUNTED:** every observed worldline appears in the census, every gate has a disposition, and each missing role or law is a typed gap. This is the old M1. It is useful even when the level is interrupted.
2. **CHARACTERIZED:** foundations are certified (or their absence is certified); every worldline has a supported role set or a scoped, evidenced unresolved status; lever dossiers and every recurring change have a causal explanation or a named unresolved residual; all current findings are grounded. Open gaps remain visible. This is a progress measure, not a claim of fullness.
3. **FILLED:** every observed worldline has a supported role set or coverage-certified INERT; no open archetype question remains in the declared observation scope; the six archetype chapters and applicable mechanic subtrees are covered; every recurring change is explained by a supported law or bounded with a falsifiable exception; all certified laws have typed form, grounding and a prospective status. A budget stop or an unresolved foundation cannot be FILLED.

The artifact declares its **scope**: observed boards, actions available, probe classes covered, conditions tested, level and life boundaries, and remaining unseen frontier. A coverage certificate states which declared classes and conditions it exhausted; it does not assert that every conceivable coordinate or hidden state was tried. FILLED is a claim about that declared scope, never about unobserved hidden states. A later contradictory observation reopens the affected dossier and changes the status. The per-world summary reports each level's status, rather than calling a world filled because one level was filled.

## 4. Starting point and gaps

The v2 rehearsal executed 2,966 environment actions in 33 active games. - **G1 NAV and answer loops:** 12 G1 gate stops in v2; the three click-only rests are repaired. The other nine require a NAV-path diagnosis. A rejected answer can still become a repair loop. WO-L1a-NAV and WO-NO-REPAIR-LOOPS address these before the library rehearsal.

- **Library feed is partial:** the v2 schema, level-entry census, early-stop path and single writer are built, but the card digest/retrieval slice is parked for strict size and paging fixes. The world/run manifest and human report are still owed. G4's lever dossiers, G5/G6 chapters and typed grammar are not yet compiled into the live library. The earlier v2 rehearsal had `world_model.json` in only 8 of 33 active games; verify the new hook in a live package.
- **Recovery and throughput:** a free-play controlled stop still ends the game on its second occurrence. Ruling 24 permits a bounded Duck-context restart in submission mode. The large prompt and long model turns must be measured, then reduced without dropping evidence. The library view must not add unbounded retrieval output or duplicate the full card.

The draft `SPEC_PHYSICS_GRAMMAR_v3` and its local code files are not yet an accepted live contract. Use the ratified v2 grammar as the baseline and integrate the draft subset only after its own tests and version decision. The gate remains the sole certified-belief writer; the census, card and grammar bridge are projections.

## 7. Measurement and cut line

Report the following per novel world and level, then aggregate without mixing independent runs:

- objects seen; objects with supported roles; objects with typed gaps; INERT coverage certificates; departed and class-changed worldlines;
- completed, unresolved and diagnostic-only gate dispositions; GATED and FREE_PLAY action counts;
- levers witnessed; each lever's dossier state (unexplained, studied, characterized or typed unresolved); forces and driven relations supported; change-explanation share and recurring residuals;
- typed, grounded and prospectively tested laws; observed wins, frozen win sentences, next-level predictions and verdicts; contradictions and reopenings;
- ACCOUNTED/CHARACTERIZED/FILLED status and the exact missing conditions;
- controller actions, Duck turns and executed actions, turn latency, time-budget cuts, restarts and time remaining.
- **Duck calls per level**, split by gate and by whether the call chose an admitted experiment, answered a semantic question, or produced no action; library target shown, target-to-experiment match, and whether the next revision gained evidence.
