## 2. WO-DESC-A contract

Add a versioned `description` chapter to each level of `virelai-world-model-v3`.

Each event contains:

- stable event ID;
- level, life, and transition ID;
- action and actor when known;
- subject track ID and optional certified role binding;
- verb;
- before and after class, footprint, anchor, bounding box, size, and normalized shape as available;
- translation, count, class, entered-site, departed-site, and pixel-rewrite deltas as applicable;
- correspondence witness and ambiguity;
- receipt and transition references;
- status `OBSERVED`;
- explanatory status `EXPLAINED`, `PARTLY_EXPLAINED`, or `UNEXPLAINED`, derived only from accepted products.

Do not recompute identity with a new matcher. Read accepted continuity output. Birth and death remain events. DEFORM and AMBIGUOUS remain honest outcomes. A class change records an event and reopens semantic role typing.

Fail-first coverage includes all nine observation verbs, multi-object transitions, resets, transient changes, class changes, record mismatch, determinism, and a test that refuses `CAUSED` without a gate receipt.

## 3. WO-DESC-B contract

## 4. G4 promotion rule

An observed event becomes causal only when an accepted G4 product binds:

- the admitted intervention;
- the lever subject;
- affected tracks or entities;
- the exact transformation event;
- conditions and limitations;
- supporting receipts;
- a prospective result when G4-C supplies one.

The dossier's anatomy, orbit, relations, and law chapters remain distinct. One G3 witness establishes the lever noun; it does not complete the G4 description.

## 5. Later roles and negatives

- G5 must separate recurring residual dynamics from ego, counter, and lever effects. Recurrence alone does not distinguish a counter from an environmental force; the schedule and trigger conditions must be explicit.
- REACTIVE-DRIVEN is earned through a measured incoming dependency.
- G6 negatives state object, board or state, action class, conditions, tested interval, and evidence.
- INERT requires an exhaustive certificate over its declared scope and excludes every other role.

## 6. Win-transfer wall

The pre-win model is frozen. Later evidence may grade or reopen a hypothesis but may not rewrite what was known before the win.
