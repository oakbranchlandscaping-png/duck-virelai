**Type:** `DOCTRINE_WORLD_MODEL_TERMINUS` · **v1** · **2026-09-24**

the project knowledge base and the repository. Nothing here is new law — the sweep's finding is

## The stated goal (Director, 2026-09-24)

> The goal is the natural-language description a human would provide: "clicking that lever
> moves that blue region down and the other one up; clicking the other lever does the opposite —
> they are moving a connected resource." The world model is the library, which is the goal. If
> the agent can explain the world like this and we then find a win state, we can define the win
> state in this detail — which allows it to become a target on the next level.

## The worked example, decomposed into its licenses

Every clause of that sentence is licensed by exactly one gate product and nothing else:

| Clause | License | Machinery |
|---|---|---|
| "clicking that lever" | G3 witness | attributed pointer intervention on a persistent entity |
| "moves that blue region down and the one up" | G4 effect law | entity-keyed `UNIFORM_TRANSFORM` with anchor delta, evidence refs |
| "the other lever does the opposite" | cross-law structure | two G4 laws with sign-negated transforms |
| "they are moving a connected resource" | coupling stratum | `ConservedTransfer` — integer-exact netting plus out-of-sample prediction; "resource" = earned conservation |

Natural language is the **projection layer, never the knowledge layer**: the dossier holds the
laws with evidence handles; the sentence renders the dossier, clause by clause, each clause a
citation and separately falsifiable. A bare model can produce the sentence on turn three,
fluently and unfounded — that is the mythology failure in its purest form, and the gates exist
so that when Duck says it, it is true a clause at a time. Contents note: "blue" is legal in
Duck's description of *this* world; it is forever illegal in the machinery. Duck may say blue;
the solver may never contain it.

## The canon lineage (swept; all currently filed, highest versions)

- **The library is the world model.** `SOLVER_BLUEPRINT_v3` §4: the library is the single sink
  where every angle on the cell-delta stream integrates; two strata — marginals (mechanics) and
  couplings (the causal map, edges between worldlines) — "comprehend every marginal and every
  coupling and you have recovered the world-law G"; roles and effects derived, never stored;
  the human-readable output is the dossier of record, the gradable artifact.
- **The sentence is the terminus.** `CONCEPT_VIRELAI_SOLVER_v2` §4: the physics grammar — nouns
  (roles) and verbs (mechanics over the invariant vocabulary), "every clause decomposable to
  delta patterns."
- **The win is written in that language.** `CONCEPT_v2` §6 / `SPEC_SOLVER_LOOPS_v2` §7, verbatim
  canon: *"A win, once observed, is described in the grammar's own language — a predicate over
  the object list, not a memorized frame."*
- **And becomes the next level's target.** Tier-1 grammar falsification, ratified: *"the
  win-predicate from level N is a live, killable hypothesis at level N+1; a miss kills the
  predicate or the clause it leans on. Content on trial; a kill is the system working."* The
  pre-registered empirical signature: if the grammar is real, later levels collapse toward
  near-free deduction — the front-loaded action curve, "a fingerprint no improvising agent can
  fake." Tier-2 stays distinct: at the game wall the wipe is absolute; only machinery transfers.
- **The Push closes the loop.** The completeness oracle: on a full library the pruned frontier
  is exhausted — WIN, or the library is *proven incomplete*, or verdict honestly withheld. "We
  did not win" becomes a falsification, never a shrug.

## The code lineage (swept; in the active tree today)

`virelai_lessons.py::LevelLesson` is the win-predicate seed already built: `boundary` (the
winning transition), `established` / `contrasts` (the same action failed earlier — the
conditionality of the win recorded as contrast), `hypotheses`, **`transfer_clauses`**,
`evidence_refs`, `limitations`; its `model_view` already renders balanced class *exchanges* —
the "connected resource" clause — from standing deltas. `virelai_win_capsule.py` preserves the
actor-independent win boundary; `compile_level_carry.py` compiles conservative level-to-level
memory from immutable post-win artifacts with an explicit `carry_rule`. What the gate campaign
adds is what these consume: today their clauses derive from raw deltas and contrasts; after
G3/G4 they derive from *laws* — the lesson's transfer clauses become citations into the dossier,
and the win predicate inherits every falsifier its cited laws carry.

## What this changes about the climb (nothing — and that is the point)

G3 supplies the nouns' causal vocabulary; G4 supplies the grammar (conditions, couplings — the
"connected resource" machinery is G4 slice S3); G7 turns the explained world into reachable
targets; G8 writes the win in the sentence's language; G9 carries the sentence forward as a
killable hypothesis. The terminus was ratified in July, seeded in Ring 8 code, and is now four
gates from being earned live. The render target for the finished world model is the
world-explanation view: the dossier projected as prose a human would nod at, graded with the
existing two-column instrument — fidelity (sentence matches the laws it cites) and correctness
(laws match the world).
