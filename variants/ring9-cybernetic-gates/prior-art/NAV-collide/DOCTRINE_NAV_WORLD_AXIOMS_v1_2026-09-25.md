## 1 · The axioms, graded by epistemic strength

The axioms come in three strengths. Keeping the strengths separate is what keeps this legal
under evidence-before-belief.

**A1 · The floor is a theorem, not an assumption.** The NAV verdict is earned (observed ego
self-motion via the observation adapter's ground-exchange derivation). Once earned, "a
traversable medium exists" is *entailed*: the ego moved, therefore something supported the
move. The floor is the set of anchors the body has stood on — golden's law verbatim ("an
anchor the body has stood on is a fact about where it can be"). Everything beyond the stood
set is *presumed* floor; the frontier press converts presumption to fact one press at a time.

**A2 · The bounds are a platform invariant.** The 64×64 grid edge exists on every world
(grounding sweep). At minimum one wall always exists: the world's own edge. This is the only
wall we get for free.

**A3 · Walls, obstacles, and targets are guaranteed QUESTIONS, never guaranteed answers.** A
world could be an empty bounded plane. What the NAV verdict guarantees is that the question
set unlocks: every refusal must be explained, every contact attributed, every roster entity
asked "terrain or lever?" The axioms guarantee the solver can never see a refusal and shrug;
they do not pre-commit the world to containing anything. Machinery is the questions; contents
are the answers.

## 2 · The NAV role lexicon

Each role is earned by a delta pattern and carries its kill condition.

| Role | Earning evidence | Dies / hands off when |
|---|---|---|
| GROUND | ego stood there (fact); frontier press landed | never — stood is stood |
| WALL | refusal at (anchor, action), stable on re-test | the refusal later yields → reborn as DOOR candidate |
| OBSTACLE | refusal whose source entity moves or changes | the blocker's own G3/G4 explains it |
| DOOR | a former WALL that yielded — a conditional refusal | not death: a G4 conditional-law handoff (candidate → freeze → discriminating demonstration) |
| TARGET | contact produced cell transfer, state change, or gains-election | its G4 law absorbs it |
| EGO | action-covariant entity (see §3 — a role, not a slot) | covariance ends (control transfer, reset identity loss) |

A WALL classification is never final: it is a standing prediction ("pressing into this yields
nothing") that every later press re-tests for free — falsification getting cheaper as we walk.

## 3 · The EGO row, corrected: a role with cardinality, not a slot

The NAV verdict guarantees only **cardinality ≥ 1**. Three shapes, each distinguishable by
recorded evidence:

- **Co-egos in unison:** multiple entities, each independently action-covariant. Discriminator:
  **differential refusal** — press so one body is blocked; if the other still moves, two egos,
  not one rigid body. A rigid formation all-moves or all-refuses.
- **Followers:** entities that covary with the ego's *motion*, not the action (a pushed block,
  a trailing mirror). The same differential-refusal probe separates them: a follower stops when
  the ego stops.
- **Latent egos (control transfer):** an entity that becomes action-covariant *after an event*.
  This is a G4 conditional law in NAV clothing — the activation event is the condition, the
  covariance flip is the observable, earned through the standard candidate → freeze →
  demonstrate rhythm. Consequence: **G1 is event-driven, not a phase that closes** — the same
  reopen doctrine already ratified for G3.

**Machinery consequences (binding on the port):**
- **The lattice is per-ego.** Golden's `Lattice` assumed one body. The port keys stood-anchor
  graph, bearings, and refused edges by controlling entity id. A control transfer opens a
  fresh lattice; it never inherits the old body's ground.
- **Attribution names the ego.** On a multi-ego world a contact is keyed to *which* ego's
  footprint intersected. Two egos, two deltas, one transition → held AMBIGUOUS, never forced.
  The existing abstention fence extends to ego-plurality; stated here so no one keys contacts
  to "the ego."
- **Economy note for counter-aware feasibility:** one action spends one counter tick but
  advances N co-ego frontiers — multi-ego worlds are cheaper to sweep per action, and the
  feasibility layer may credit this.

## 4 · The collide definition (Director-ruled): footprint occupation, never adjacency

> **"Collide" means: attempt to occupy the cell-site footprint of the target object.**

This eliminates the adjacent/on problem by construction. Both ends have exact prior art:

- **Intent side** (`collide_plan.py`): a contact attempt is a press whose *predicted landing
  footprint intersects the target's footprint*. Adjacency is never the goal; flush-alongside
  is not an attempt.
- **Evidence side** (`collide_drive.py`, paid for live): "arrival is cell transfer, never
  adjacency; a displace with no transfer never registers as reached."

**The outcome partition (exhaustive; every branch is evidence):**

| Outcome | Reading |
|---|---|
| ENTERED | ego cells now inside the target footprint |
| DISPLACED | target moved — a push mechanic candidate |
| TRANSFORMED | target or ego changed class — consumption/trigger candidate |
| REFUSED | no delta — the refusal IS the contact (R-I); types toward WALL/OBSTACLE |

"On" is a claim about footprint intersection *in the record*, never proximity in the eye. A
one-cell-away ego has attempted nothing and learned nothing.

## 5 · The goal-card grammar for NAV worlds

The terminus sentence for a NAV world, every clause licensed: *"I am the small shape; the dark
region is floor; the bordered regions are walls — pressing into them does nothing; that moving
thing blocks me; touching that object drained the bar."* GROUND licensed by stood anchors,
WALL by refused edges, TARGET by attributed contact, couplings by G4. Duck's per-turn job on a
NAV world is not "figure out navigation" — it is "read the map we've earned and say what the
next press tests." The axioms are the grammar of the map. (Contents note: descriptive words
like "dark" are legal in Duck's rendering of *this* world only; never in machinery.)
