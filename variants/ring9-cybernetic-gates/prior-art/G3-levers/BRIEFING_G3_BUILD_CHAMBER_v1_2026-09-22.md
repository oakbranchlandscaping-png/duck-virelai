## 1 · What G3 is

G3 is the discovery half of the lever lifecycle: which interventions produce effects outside the
acted locus. It is **per-entity work, not a global phase** — the Director's ruling is that G3 and
G4 are one work walked object by object: the sweep discovers an entity's activity, that entity's
G3 runs (witness, first-order typing) to G3-CLOSED, and its G4 (causal expansion) becomes READY,
while the sweep keeps hunting and any newly discovered lever enters at its own G3. The world
model this builds is the dossier: every entity gets the same questions; the gates are the lawful
order of the questions; the machinery is the question set and the contents are the answers.

The build is **a port, not an invention.** The mining pass found every layer already built and
proven at least once — most twice. Your job is transcription with provenance, against the ruled
contract, through the existing gate router. The genuinely new surface is thin: the G3 gate state
machine, the abstention route to G5, and the coverage-first scheduling policy.

## 4 · Disciplines that bind every slice

Diagnostic-before-fix; test-before-fix; the amendment-landing-status pre-check at your own
anchor as the WO's first premise. Tests are production-shaped: drive the real gate machine and
write artifact rows exactly as `tool_agent.py` writes them — the G2 evaluator's test file
(`tests/test_g2_checkpoint_evaluation.py`) is the working example of the pattern. Additive only:
the working tree carries unrelated in-flight material; preserve it. Sandbox pytest only; the
Director runs anything live. No game name, color, position, or mechanic enters code, tests, or
prompts — the sweep is game-free by construction and stays that way. Budget-awareness is a
requirement, not an optimization: enumeration must be schedulable within a level's ~5n budget,
with the certificate recording what the budget forbade.

## 5 · Halts — surface, never patch

Escalate rather than improvise on any of these: the partial autonomous mover (no archetype
exists; G3 abstains via `FILED_TO_G5` and `unattributed_residue` — do not type it); a bookkeeping
process the G2 pair-exchange archetype cannot represent (`REPRESENTATION_GAP`, never absence);
any question the theses do not answer (that is a CE/Director ruling, not a work-order
improvisation); and any file the mine cites that is absent or different at your anchor (a
finding to report, never content to assume). A halt is information. A session that ends in an
honest "examined — no change" finished its work.
