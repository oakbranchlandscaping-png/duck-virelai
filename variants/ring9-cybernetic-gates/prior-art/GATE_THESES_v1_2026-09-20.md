# Gate theses — the goal of every gate, outline form

**Type:** `GATE_THESES` · **v1** · **2026-09-20**
**Status:** working statement for the gate-by-gate discussion with the Director; each thesis is
amended in place by a higher version when its gate's discussion rules otherwise. Order starts at
the frontier. Every gate shares one rhythm — candidate from evidence, Duck's explanation with
competing model and falsifier, frozen prospective prediction, demonstration, versioned product —
and one grammar: all mechanics are cell-delta patterns; interpretations stay falsifiable and
evidence-linked.

---

## G3 · Levers — the frontier

**Question:** which interventions produce effects outside the acted locus — on what subject, by
what transformation, under what conditions.
**Product:** a versioned `LeverLaw` per demonstrated lever (trigger, acted subject, affected
subject, transformation, conditions, competing model, falsifier, limitations).
**Refusals:** a counter tick is HUD delta, never a lever effect (the mask and locked-counter
monitor enforce this — why G3 could not precede G2). An effect on the ego or at the contact
point is G4's, not a lever; the lever's signature is the third party. A change with no
attributable intervention is a force candidate — G3 abstains toward G5 and never types it (the
partial-autonomous-mover halt must be designed in, not rediscovered).
**Law:** LEVER is ∃ — one witness, permanent, silence never retracts. INERT is ∀ — never proven;
the sweep ends only on enumeration-completeness, never on a run of nulls.
**Open rulings for the discussion:** (a) is the coverage sweep G3-private or the shared
intervention spine that G4 and G6 later read from; (b) Duck's role during the sweep —
interpreter only, or allowed to reorder the schedule with an explicit recorded reason.

## G4 · Reactions and terrain

**Question:** how do entities and terrain respond to the ego's motion, contact, activation, and
to lever effects — traversal, refusal, collision, carry, consumption, transformation.
**Product:** scoped `ReactionLaw`s bound to the tested boundary and conditions; the ground/wall
distinction (what the ego walks on versus what refuses it) as constructions, not measurements.
**Refusals:** a ground's deltas are the ego's own and never lever evidence; a refusal is
evidence — the refusal that types a wall IS the contact, not a failed probe. Boundary presses
(death, level-up, respawn) are events, not moves, and never enter reaction laws.
**Known precondition from the archive:** on an embodied world the background/medium must be
named before lever reading is trustworthy — the ground deforms on every move, and until it is
named every displacement reads as an effect.

## G5 · Environmental forces

**Question:** what changes without a contemporaneous intervention or a known lever/reaction
driver — and is it truly autonomous or state-conditioned.
**Product:** a `ForceLaw`, or an evidence-backed `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
**Refusals:** the counter is not a force (already quotiented out); a *sometimes*-mover is not
forced into lever, counter, or inert — this gate owns the archetype the whole archive records as
missing and load-bearing. The one standing amendment owed: the force's observable is
state-conditioned, not "autonomous."
**Note:** G5 is the first gate whose central archetype has no settled prior answer; expect its
discussion to produce a genuine ruling, not a port list.

## G6 · Scoped inertness

**Question:** which tested subjects failed to respond, under exactly which actions, states, and
conditions.
**Product:** interaction coverage certificates — what was aimed, what the world offered, what
fired, what was never tried — supporting bounded inertness claims.
**Refusals:** INERT is PROVISIONAL, always; repetition is a count, not an exhaustion; where the
platform offers no aimable action, inertness is unassertable and the certificate says so rather
than staying silent. Negative evidence from G1 or G3 never proves universal inertness.

## G7 · Counter-masked novelty frontier

**Question:** which world configurations have not been observed once bookkeeping noise is
quotiented out — and how do we reach one.
**Product:** the persistent masked state archive as a machine — (state, key) → state — with
visited/frontier addresses, reachability edges labeled by exact interventions, and per-state
UNTRIED keys; exploration selects reachable novelty driven by unresolved physics questions.
**Refusals:** a changed raw frame is not novelty (counter ticks mint no new states — the
quotient's whole point). A contradiction — same (state, key), two landings — is reported, never
averaged: hidden state or mask leak, and telling those apart is a later question. Saturation is
a verdict (`EXHAUSTED_NO_WIN`), not a failure. An already-seen winning state would already have
won — so exploration seeks unvisited configurations without guessing which one wins.

## G8 · Observed-win explanation

**Question:** which observed transition produced the terminal event, and how does the learned
physics describe it.
**Product:** an evidence-linked win explanation bound to a real terminal transition — the win
capsule: boundary, preceding state, action, active laws, participating entities.
**Refusals:** the win is never guessed and never sought — it is observed by the play loop during
exploration. A win during an early gate is preserved as an event and does not retroactively
regrade the gate that was active. No explanation ships that does not point at a recorded
terminal transition.

## G9 · Cross-level transfer

**Question:** which laws — ego, counter, lever, reaction, goal — survive into the next level,
and as what.
**Product:** the observed goal explanation converted to a falsifiable forward hypothesis;
every carried law explicitly preserved, specialized, reopened, or rejected on the new level's
evidence. Conservative carry from immutable post-win artifacts only.
**Refusals:** carry is hypothesis, never truth; nothing transfers silently; a carried law that
the new frame contradicts dies exactly as a native one would.

## Sealed gates (reference theses — the live build already implements these)

**G0 · Action surface.** Which opaque actions exist and which require coordinates — exact
interface metadata acknowledged with zero semantic claims and a stated epistemic limit; the
archetype prior (embodied / pointer / hybrid) is interface axiom, not world knowledge.

**G1 · Ego.** Where and how Duck directly influences the world — a versioned `EgoLaw`: pointer
worlds close from the interface axiom the moment the surface answers the question; embodied and
hybrid worlds require behavioral localization and a prospective demonstration. No target,
counter, or remote-effect claim rides in on the ego.

**G2 · Counter.** Which visible process is bookkeeping — the conserved two-object exchange,
prospective rate confirmation, min/max boundary lock with an observed consequence, reversible
mask, and the retroactive world-address quotient. The gate stays open until the boundary
consequence is seen; rate recurrence alone never locks it.
